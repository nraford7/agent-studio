# Team Charter

The Team Charter confirms the **organization** — not the people — for a durable
result. It is the first step of Stage 8, after roles have passed the evidence gate
and before any candidate is presented.

**Durability gate.** The charter applies only to the two durable outputs: a
**reusable staffed workflow** or a **standing team or panel**. The lightweight
outputs — an in-house playbook, a solo specialist, or a one-off panel — skip the
charter and run the lean lifecycle (`SKILL.md`).

**Charter approval is never candidate approval.** Approving the charter approves
the shape of the team — its roles, rules, and budget. It says nothing about who
fills the seats. The two are separate gates, in this order: approve the
organization, then meet and hire the people. If the user rejects the charter,
revise the organization and do not present candidates yet.

Write the charter to `agent-studio-out/<slug>/team-charter.md`. Present it to the
user in the warm, plain voice of `references/voice.md`.

## What the charter records

- **Purpose and durability** — what this team is for, and which durable output it
  is (reusable staffed workflow or standing team).
- **Methodology** — the selected kernel version and overlays
  (`methodology-selection.md` from Stage 3).
- **Open specialist roles** — each with its evidence-gate conclusion and the proof
  it owes or has met.
- **In-house work** — the jobs assigned to regular agents, tools, or deterministic
  automation, with their playbooks.
- **Topology, sequencing, handoffs** — who works in parallel, who is sequential,
  what passes between them.
- **Exposure and convergence rules** — who sees whose work, when, and why
  (progressive exposure; kernel rule 4).
- **Critic and combination logic** — whether a critic seat exists and how outputs
  are combined (`references/synthesis-modes.md`).
- **Model constraints** — where different model families are required and why
  (high-stakes normative work).
- **Budgets** — time, context, and iteration budgets (kernel rule 10).
- **Human checkpoints** — the consequential decisions where the user is consulted.
- **Coverage and deliberate exclusions** — which axes the team covers and which it
  knowingly does not.
- **Required artifacts** — what each phase must leave on disk.
- **Promotion, review, and retirement policy** — how the team is reviewed, when it
  is promoted or retired.

## Charter checklist

- [ ] Purpose and durability stated.
- [ ] Kernel version and overlays named.
- [ ] Every open role carries its evidence conclusion and proof status.
- [ ] In-house jobs are separated from specialist roles.
- [ ] Topology, exposure, and combination rules are explicit for any parallel work.
- [ ] Budgets and human checkpoints set.
- [ ] Deliberate exclusions recorded (what the team is not built to see).
- [ ] The user approved the organization BEFORE any candidate was shown.
